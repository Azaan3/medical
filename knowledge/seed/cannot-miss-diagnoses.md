# Cannot-miss diagnoses (emergency screening)

Licensed physicians must rule out these before anchoring on benign causes. Local protocols override this list.

## Chest pain
- Acute coronary syndrome (STEMI/NSTEMI)
- Aortic dissection
- Pulmonary embolism
- Tension pneumothorax
- Esophageal rupture (Boerhaave)

**Red flags:** tearing pain to back, hypotension, unequal pulses, new murmur, SpO₂ drop, hemoptysis, syncope.

## Headache
- Subarachnoid hemorrhage
- Meningitis / encephalitis
- Intracranial hemorrhage / mass
- Cervical artery dissection
- Acute angle-closure glaucoma
- Cavernous sinus thrombosis

**Red flags:** thunderclap onset, fever + neck stiffness, focal neuro deficit, papilledema, postpartum, anticoagulation, immunocompromised.

## Abdominal pain
- Ruptured ectopic pregnancy
- Mesenteric ischemia
- Ruptured AAA
- Bowel obstruction / perforation
- Acute pancreatitis (severe)
- Testicular/ovarian torsion

**Red flags:** peritonitis, GI bleeding, pregnancy, age >50 with new pain, AF on anticoagulation, disproportionate pain vs exam.

## Fever without clear source
- Sepsis / septic shock
- Meningococcemia
- Endocarditis
- Deep space infection (epidural, nec fasc)
- Malaria (travel history)

## Shortness of breath
- PE, pneumothorax, anaphylaxis, ACS, status asthmaticus, ARDS, methemoglobinemia

## Syncope
- Arrhythmia, PE, aortic stenosis, SAH, GI bleed, ectopic rupture

## General actions when red flag present
1. Stabilize ABCs and vitals
2. Do not delay time-critical tests (ECG, lactate, pregnancy test, CT as indicated)
3. Escalate per local sepsis / stroke / MI / PE pathways
