# Chest pain — rapid assessment pathway

## Within 10 minutes
- ABCs, vitals, SpO₂, IV access if unstable
- **12-lead ECG** — if STEMI, activate reperfusion pathway per local protocol
- Aspirin (if not contraindicated) when ACS suspected
- Brief HPI: onset, character, radiation, exertional, pleuritic, positional

## Differential by likelihood (pre-test — refine with data)
**Life-threatening first**
1. ACS / unstable angina
2. Pulmonary embolism
3. Aortic dissection
4. Tension pneumothorax
5. Esophageal rupture

**Other common**
- Pericarditis, myocarditis
- Pneumonia, pleurisy
- GERD / spasm (diagnosis of exclusion in ED)

## Useful tests
- Troponin (serial per protocol), D-dimer only with appropriate pre-test probability for PE
- CXR
- CT angiography: PE protocol or aortic dissection when suspicion high

## PE red flags
Sudden dyspnea, pleuritic pain, hemoptysis, recent immobilization/surgery, OCP, cancer, unilateral leg swelling, tachycardia out of proportion.

## Dissection red flags
Tearing pain to back, pulse/BP differential, new diastolic murmur, neurologic deficits, widened mediastinum on CXR.

## Documentation
Record explicit ruled-out cannot-miss conditions when dispositioning home.
