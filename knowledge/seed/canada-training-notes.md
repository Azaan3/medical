# Canada — training mode reference (not clinical clearance)

This app runs in **training/simulation mode** for Canada. Verify all content against current Canadian sources before any future clinical use.

## Regulatory context (high level)
- **Health Canada** regulates software as a medical device when intended for diagnosis/treatment decisions.
- **PIPEDA** (federal) and provincial laws (e.g. **PHIPA** in Ontario, **HIA** in Alberta) govern personal health information.
- **CPSO / provincial colleges** set practice standards for physicians; trainees work under supervision.

## Canadian guideline sources to add (upload to knowledge/)
- Health Canada publications
- Canadian Thoracic Society / Canadian Cardiovascular Society guidelines
- Choosing Wisely Canada
- Provincial emergency department standards (e.g. Ontario MOH)
- UpToDate is licensed — do not scrape; use institution subscriptions separately

## Training case conventions
- Use fictitious names: "Patient A", "58M trainee case 12"
- No real health card numbers (OHIP, RAMQ, PHN, etc.)
- Document learning objectives: "rule out PE", "practice sepsis workup"

## Cannot-miss (same clinical principles, local pathways differ)
Apply the same emergency screening as global seed content, but disposition and investigations must follow local Canadian hospital protocols when in supervised clinical training — never via this uncertified app on real patients.
